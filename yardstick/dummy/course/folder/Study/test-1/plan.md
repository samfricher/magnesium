## 5. Method

1. Sit a paper under the stated conditions, timed, with the permitted aids only.
2. Mark it strictly against the key's mark scheme straight after.
3. Log each part that lost marks in `gap-log.md`, once, with a tag and a type.
4. Repair at the depth the gap needs: tighten, repair or teach.
5. Redo each gap cold, from a blank page, on three different days.
6. Sit the next paper. The plan is re-planned after every marked paper.

Rating: mixed. Ramp: Diagnostic, MockA, old test 1.
