# Exercise book, Unit 2

## A. Expected value

2.1 A raffle ticket wins 50 dollars with probability 0.02, 5 dollars with probability 0.10 and nothing otherwise. Find the expected winnings.

2.2 A fair four-sided die shows 1, 2, 3 or 4. Write the table of X and find E[X].

2.3 X takes the values −1, 0 and 3 with probabilities 0.3, 0.5 and 0.2. Find E[X] and E[X²].

2.4 A taxi charges 4 dollars plus 2 dollars per kilometre. The trip length K has E[K] = 7. Find the expected fare, and say which rule you used.

## B. Variance

2.5 X takes the values 0, 1 and 4 with probabilities 0.5, 0.3 and 0.2. Find Var(X) from the definition and again with the shortcut.

2.6 Show that Var(X) = E[X²] − (E[X])², starting from the definition.

2.7 Var(X) = 9. Find Var(2X + 5) and SD(2X + 5).

2.8 A thermometer reads Celsius with variance 4. Fahrenheit is F = 1.8C + 32. Find Var(F), and explain why the 32 does not appear.

## C. Named distributions

2.9 A quiz has 8 multiple-choice questions with 4 options each, and a student guesses every one. Name the distribution of the number correct and check its three conditions.

2.10 For the quiz in 2.9, find the mean and the variance of the number correct.

2.11 Find P(X = 2) for X Binomial(5, 0.4).

2.12 A basketball player makes each free throw with probability 0.25, independently. Find the expected number of throws up to and including her first make.

2.13 The player in 2.12 has missed her first 3 throws. Find the expected number of further throws until her first make, and name the property you used.

2.14 Say whether each count is binomial, geometric or neither: (a) heads in 20 tosses; (b) tosses until the first head; (c) aces in 5 cards dealt without replacement.

## D. Indicators and sums

2.15 Ten people each pick a number from 1 to 10 at random. Use indicators to find the expected number of people who pick 7.

2.16 Deal 13 cards from a 52 card deck. Find the expected number of hearts.

2.17 n people put their hats in a pile and each takes one at random. Find the expected number who get their own hat.

2.18 X and Y are independent with variances 3 and 5. Find Var(X + Y) and Var(X − Y).
