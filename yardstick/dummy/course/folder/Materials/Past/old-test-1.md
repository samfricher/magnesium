# Test 1 from an earlier term

50 minutes. Closed book. A calculator is allowed. Show your work.

## Problem 1 (8 marks)

X takes the values 0, 2 and 5 with probabilities 0.4, 0.5 and 0.1.

a) Find E[X].
b) Find Var(X).

## Problem 2 (6 marks)

A machine makes a faulty part with probability 0.1, independently for each part. A box holds 20 parts.

a) Name the distribution of the number of faulty parts in a box, with its parameters.
b) Find its mean and variance.

## Problem 3 (6 marks)

Var(X) = 4.

a) Find Var(3X + 2).
b) Explain in one sentence why the 2 does not matter.
