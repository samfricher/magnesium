# A sitting is a learning activity outside the one-task rule

The teaching policy issues one task and waits. That gives scaffolding and immediate feedback, but no honest measure of what a learner can do alone under exam conditions. We decided that a sitting, a timed set of questions worked away from the conversation and returned whole for marking, is a distinct learning activity that the one-task rule does not govern. The one-task rule still applies to practice inside the conversation.

## Consequences

- A sitting loses in-chat scaffolding and immediate feedback. It gains breadth and an unassisted baseline that re-plans the route.
- The marked sitting, not a single task, is the unit that produces gaps, repair blocks and the next plan change.
- Answer-bearing keys stay private until the sitting is returned, as `MAGNESIUM.md` already requires for answer-bearing material.
