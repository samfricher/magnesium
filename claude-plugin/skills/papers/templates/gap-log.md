# Gap log: <target>

One row per lost mark. A row closes after three cold passes on three different days. A pass is a correct answer from a blank page, without help. The first redo comes at least 1 day after the repair, the second at least 3 days after the first pass, and the third at least 7 days after the second. If the assessment comes sooner, the redos still fit before it, each on its own day. A pass counts only once the row's Next redo has come. A later cold sitting that passes a question fully containing the row's tag counts as one pass, except for an S row. A failed redo sets the row back to open and clears its passes. Repair it again, and the spacing starts over. Marks and scores live here; the learner record holds the interpreted state.

Status: open, 1 of 3, 2 of 3 or closed. Passes: the date and result of each redo, such as 2026-10-17 pass, or 2026-10-20 pass (MockA Q3) for a pass in a sitting. A failed redo starts the list again with that failure, such as 2026-10-24 fail. Next redo: the date the next redo is due, set after the repair and after each pass, and empty once the row closes.

Type: K did not know, S slow, N notation or missing step, A arithmetic, R misread.
